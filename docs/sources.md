## Case 2: Sent money to a scammer, via Safaricom M-PESA

**Last verified:** 21 Oct 2026

| Action | Official channel | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Request a reversal by SMS | Forward the full M-PESA confirmation message to **456**. A reply from "Reversal" confirms the request; updates arrive within about 2 hours. | Safaricom M-PESA Reversal page |
| Request a reversal on WhatsApp | Chat with Zuri on **0722000100**: send "Hello", choose option 2 (M-PESA Help and Reversal), then option 1 (Reverse). Needs the transaction code or full message, and the M-PESA PIN to authenticate. | Safaricom M-PESA Reversal page |
| Request a reversal in an app | Reverse transaction icon in the M-PESA app or mySafaricom app. Only for payments made in that same app. | Safaricom M-PESA Reversal page |
| Report the fraudster | Forward the fraudster's number or message to **333**. | Safaricom Fraud Awareness page |
| Speak to a person | Customer care **100** or **200**, or visit a Safaricom shop. | Safaricom Secure M-PESA page |
| Genuine Safaricom calls | Safaricom says it only contacts customers from **0722000000**. | Safaricom Fraud Awareness page |

**Important limits (from the same pages)**
- A reversal depends on Safaricom contacting the recipient and getting their consent, so it is not guaranteed.
- The reversal page states no time limit. The M-PESA Customer Terms (clause 8.14) say a reversal claim for an erroneous transfer must be made within one month of the transaction date, and the recipient must not have redeemed the funds.
- The pages do not describe how to block a compromised M-PESA account or lost SIM. The documented route is customer care on 100 or 200, or a Safaricom shop.
- The reversal page lists the transaction code or full message as the required information.

## Escalation: where to go if nobody replies

**Last verified:** 22 Oct 2026. These facts are used by the case tracker (`backend/escalation.json`).

### Banks (Central Bank of Kenya)
- Complain to the bank first, formally, through any available channel.
- The bank must acknowledge within **48 hours** and undertakes to resolve within **7 days**. If it will take more than seven business days, it must tell you and give a timeline.
- If unresolved or unsatisfactory, write to the Central Bank of Kenya with full details, the bank's response, and copies of all correspondence.
- CBK cannot consider matters already before the courts.
- **Gap:** the CBK document gives no address, email, phone number or form. Find the current contact details on centralbank.go.ke before you cite any.
- Source: https://www.centralbank.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Customer-Complaints-Handling-Mechanism.pdf

### Safaricom M-PESA (M-PESA Customer Terms and Conditions)
- Clause 15.1: contact Safaricom Customer Care for disputes. The terms mention "line 234 or any other line as advised by Safaricom". Safaricom's other pages list 100 or 200, so confirm the current line before you cite one.
- Clause 15.3: an unresolved dispute can go to the Safaricom manager responsible for M-PESA operations, and "you are also at liberty to refer any dispute that is not resolved to your satisfaction to the Central Bank of Kenya".
- Clause 15.4: disputes not resolved by Customer Care can go to a Kenyan court.
- Clause 8.14: a reversal claim for an erroneous transfer must be made **within one month** of the transaction date, and the recipient must not have redeemed the funds.
- **Gap:** the terms set no deadline for Safaricom to resolve a complaint.
- Source: https://www.safaricom.co.ke/media-center-landing/terms-and-conditions/m-pesa-customer-terms-conditions

### Telecom service complaints (Communications Authority of Kenya)
- Report to the service provider first and keep evidence; use the provider's internal escalation.
- Then complain in writing to the Communications Authority within **six months** of the incident, with proof of the earlier complaint. Filing is free.
- Channels listed by CA: online form at www.ca.go.ke, emails complaints@ca.go.ke and chukuahatua@ca.go.ke.
- CA says it acknowledges within **3 days** and responds substantively within **10 days**.
- CA does not handle matters that are in court or concluded.
- **Note:** this applies to telecom service complaints. It was not verified that M-PESA payment disputes go to CA, so the tracker sends M-PESA disputes to Safaricom and then CBK, as the M-PESA terms say.
- Source: https://www.ca.go.ke/node/243
